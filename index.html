ProbXcape

3D Game · EdTech · 2026

A browser-based 3D horror escape room that teaches simple probability. Players are trapped in a fog-filled school corridor and must clear three classrooms — easy, moderate, hard — answering questions to earn digits of the exit code. Get too many wrong and something starts chasing you. Supabase handles accounts, leaderboards and per-question analytics, with a teacher dashboard for tracking a class.

07

Citrus Classifier

Research · Mobile ML · 2026

Dalandan and Calamansi are hard to tell apart at market — both green, both round — so anything working from colour struggles under changing light. This reads texture instead: grayscale images through a custom CNN running alongside a Local Binary Pattern layer, learning the rind rather than the hue. Undergraduate research comparing feature representations to find which best separated the two fruits from each other and from lookalike objects, shipped as an offline Flutter app that classifies on the phone.

  • Flutter
  • Dart
  • TensorFlow
  • TensorFlow Lite
  • CNN + LBP
  • UMAP
