Everyone’s photos from the party in one place, with no internet involved. Guests join the local network, scan a QR code, pick a name, and every photo they take lands on everybody else’s screen a second later. Nothing to install and no signal needed, it works in a venue with no Wi-Fi and no data. At the end of the night the whole gallery is handed over and wiped.

An integer crossword adventure for Grade 7 learners. Fill the missing numbers in connected equations, use clues to understand sign rules, and earn coins for hints. Relax mode introduces the puzzles, Expert adds timed rankings, and Battle lets groups race on a shared grid. A separate teacher dashboard manages student codes, tracks progress, and exports results for classroom research. Built as an installable web app with offline solo play.
