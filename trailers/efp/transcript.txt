00:00 — EFP SUPPLY CHAIN
When fresh produce leaves the warehouse, the work is only half done. On consignment, a delivery is not yet a sale. E F P Supply Chain keeps those numbers connected.

00:13 — 01 / THE BIG PICTURE
The dashboard brings stock levels, upcoming expiry dates, and unresolved discrepancies into one view. Deliveries by branch and recent movements show where the stock is going.

00:25 — 02 / TRACE THE STOCK
Each production batch has its own number and expiry date. Remaining quantities stay visible, so staff can check which batch is available before recording the next delivery.

00:37 — 03 / RECORD THE JOURNEY
Production adds stock. Deliveries reduce it. Warehouse and transit losses are recorded separately. Every movement keeps the product, quantity, and responsible user together, with delivery references where needed.

00:52 — 04 / CLOSE THE GAP
Reconciliation compares the delivery with the branch's reported sales. Fifteen delivered and thirteen sold leaves two units to resolve. Accounting can document a write off or correct an entry, keeping the reason on record.

01:07 — 05 / SEE THE PATTERN
The consignment summary groups delivered and sold quantities by product and branch. Reports turn that movement history into figures the team can review together.

01:19 — 06 / KEEP RESPONSIBILITY CLEAR
Warehouse, sales, and accounting have different permissions. The audit log records who changed what, making stock decisions easier to trace when questions come up.

From warehouse intake to branch sales, E F P Supply Chain gives every stock movement a place in the ledger. Built with Django and My S Q L.
